# S02 — intent, time and attention

## F002 queued-wakeup increment — 10 October 2026

The existing wakeup actor now owns an additive job generation, captured by each
new run. Pause/cancel invalidates unclaimed work; resume never rehabilitates the
old generation. Source, producer-audience and trigger-ancestry checks still apply
after persistence. Running records survive invalidation for their original
completion receipt, while forgotten payloads are scrubbed independently.

Stop evidence is deliberately narrower than cancellation intent. Only a stopped
job explicitly recorded as never admitted can report `fenced=true, settled=true`.
Any admitted or legacy history reports both false, including after aggregate
completion or bounded history expiry. False means unavailable proof, not proof
that a process remains running. Run status is not a model/tool/delivery receipt;
unknown outcomes cannot be retried or relabeled settled. This increment does not
mount an IntentPort or attest full F002.

June receives these limits in interaction, execution-worker, event-decision and
notification-only instructions. Her existing scoped wakeup inspect/pause/resume/
cancel actions expose the evidence; automated notifications gain no new tools.
Quiet hours, urgency, missed-run behavior and current permissions are unchanged.

Disposable local verification uses the real Rivet host and engine with controlled
model/channel adapters, never paid inference or a real external message. Stop
before dispatch prevents a run. Stop during provider wait reports false/false;
the unchanged legacy consumer demonstrably can still send afterward. Stop after
that acceptance does not undo it, acknowledge a fence or permit another claim.
Transport ambiguity remains unknown. A separate actor-boundary probe rejects
trigger-only deletion, missing native audience and deletion during persistence,
while accepting the original raw completion.

A first admission withheld during its own save is durably cancelled before
returning false, so a resumed repeating job can accept later valid events.
Overlapping claims share that admission result. An earlier positive admission
is never retired by this path; its original consumer still owns settlement.
The sticky admission evidence stays conservative even for a withheld claim.

An initial provider-settlement probe exposed another existing limitation: legacy
notifications call `model.reply`, not the settlement-aware `beginReply`. Aggregate
completion cannot certify provider settlement. C02 owns that root integration;
this increment does not hide the limitation or treat inspection as attestation.

State reads are compatible with legacy missing fields, without a migration or
rewriting history. This is not permission to downgrade to a pre-increment writer:
old writers do not maintain the new generation/admission evidence. Preserve the
deployment controller's source-compatibility gate and forward-recovery policy;
do not add a `rollbackSafe` override for an old writer.

Remaining F002 work includes C02 root/descendant admission and abort propagation,
S02's leased activity/catalog stop hooks, S14 workflow/research and S19 coding
integration, and real live acceptance through F001's truthful ledger. Optional
host-only intent bindings carry frozen provenance, never new authority. A shared
async `begin/current` RPC is not the same-owner synchronous dispatch fence.
F003/F005 are not claimed by this wakeup increment; deterministic previews may
proceed without selecting new D7 quiet/catch-up policy.

## Exact-assignment activity hooks — local integration boundary

The activity actor exposes `stop(assignment)` and `stopStatus(assignment)`;
`ActivityStopReceipt` derives from the published K2 stop return type. The catalog
exposes `stop(host, assignment)` and `stopStatus(host, assignment)` with optional
`SessionHost.stopActivity` / `activityStopStatus` callbacks. C02 owns registration
of conversation `activityStop` / `activityStopStatus` and the `ActivityCatalog`
mapping. These hooks alone do not mount an IntentPort or expose a cancellation
tool to June, and do not allocate or infer an intent reference.

A stop closes the existing local turn synchronously, saves it, then signals its
original provider. Pending catalog calls and the original dispatch carrier keep
the receipt false until they actually retire. Durable prepare/apply markers
prevent an uncertain RPC response from becoming permission to repeat it after
restart. Legacy missing tracking is unknown, not first admission. Existing
historical acknowledgements remain available after revocation. A revoked control
may complete its saved receipt-only archival/acknowledgement without new inference,
dispatch or delivery; stopping an active local turn does not authorize fresh
catalog preparation. Missing legacy receipts remain held after revocation rather
than becoming an empty acknowledged turn. Existing saved control and delivery
receipts may still finish their bookkeeping without replaying uncertain effects.

Raw provider/tool/delivery unknowns, incomplete controls, best-effort typing and
unaccounted native/worker descendants remain false/false. Known accepted sends
stay recorded, not undone. The catalog does not turn a successful native publish
or worker summary into descendant cancellation evidence. No new scheduler, store,
queue, workflow journal step, generation backfill or funding gate is introduced.
The published Rivet lifecycle, serializer, timeout and failure wrappers remain
owned by their original publisher. Local stop tracking is forward-writer state;
an older writer cannot maintain it or certify a stop.

New asynchronous stop/status and catalog occupancy helpers run on the injected
process `effectRuntime`, using the original actor/step abort signal. The public
Rivet/Promise boundary and journal order stay unchanged. Revocation and stop
barriers are registered synchronously before Effect execution. Raw persistence
starts only inside the admitted uninterruptible save-to-stop region. Cancellation
during runtime initialization starts no raw save and retains a failed barrier;
a later explicit stop may retry that flush only after every predecessor's pending
save settles. A failed replacement cannot sever the earlier occupancy, including
when calls have different original host lifetimes. Admitted raw waits are uninterruptible:
cancelling an Effect waiter or disposing its runtime
cannot retire an unresolved RPC or turn it into a successful stop. This can
delay shutdown while a raw call remains unresolved. Missing runtime wiring
rejects catalog work and returns no positive stop receipt; it never creates a
fallback runtime. C01/C02 own production injection and shutdown composition.

This boundary is independent of C02 root allocation. The agreed root is frozen
at first fresh authenticated source admission; replay/resume and unrelated new
messages must not mint or supersede that root. Wakeup generations remain local
to their existing owner. Full F002 and F003 acceptance remain separate.
